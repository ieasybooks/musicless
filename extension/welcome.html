nomusic is ready

  1. Reload any YouTube tabs that were open before you installed nomusic.
  2. Play a YouTube video. nomusic removes its background music automatically. At the start, and after jumping to a new part, the video pauses for a moment while nomusic prepares the audio.
  3. The nomusic on button on the video turns it off (and on again) for that video. The nomusic button in the browser toolbar has the setting for starting automatically.

What to expect

  • Voices are kept: speech and singing. The instruments are removed. Some quiet music can remain.
  • Everything happens on this computer, on one processor core. Nothing is uploaded, and nomusic collects no data. While it is working it uses about 600 MB of memory.
  • If nomusic can't remove the music from a video, the video stays paused and silent, and nomusic asks whether to retry or play the original audio.

Limits

  • It works on YouTube in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on a computer, not on phones.
  • Copy-protected videos, such as rented movies, cannot be processed.
  • A live stream watched right at its live point keeps pausing; a little behind it plays smoothly.
