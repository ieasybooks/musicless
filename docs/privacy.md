# nomusic privacy policy

nomusic is a browser extension that removes background music from YouTube
videos on your computer.

**nomusic collects no data.** It has no servers, makes no network requests,
and sends nothing anywhere: no audio, no browsing history, no identifiers, no
usage statistics.

## What it does with the audio

On YouTube pages, nomusic reads the audio that YouTube's own player has
already loaded into the browser, separates the voices from the music in the
browser, and plays the voices. The audio stays in the browser's memory while
the page is open (at most about 16 MB per video) and is never saved to disk,
uploaded or shared.

## What it stores

One setting, whether nomusic starts automatically, in the browser's extension
storage. If you sign in to your browser with sync turned on, the browser may
sync this setting between your own devices.

## Permissions

- **storage:** to remember that setting.
- **Running on youtube.com, m.youtube.com and youtube-nocookie.com:** to read
  the audio of the videos you watch there and show the nomusic button. nomusic
  does not run on any other site.
