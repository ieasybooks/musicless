**Description:**

```text
Watch YouTube™ in Safari with less background music while keeping speech and singing. Musicless is for anyone who wants to avoid music for religious or personal reasons, or simply prefers to hear the voices clearly.

Set up once in Safari
• On Mac: Open Musicless, click Open Safari Settings, turn on Musicless, and allow youtube.com, m.youtube.com, and youtube-nocookie.com.
• On iPhone & iPad: Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Musicless, turn on Allow Extension, and allow youtube.com, m.youtube.com, and youtube-nocookie.com.
• Choose Turn on Musicless when first prompted. If YouTube was already open, reload the page.

Watch as usual
• Give each video a few seconds to start while you see Removing music. Jumping ahead may cause a short pause.
• Tap or click the crossed-out music note in the video player to Restore original sound, and select it again to Remove music.
• To choose videos manually, open Musicless from Safari’s Extensions menu and turn off Remove music by default. The player button always overrides just the current video.
• Works on regular videos, Shorts, live streams, and supported embedded YouTube players across Mac, iPhone, and iPad—including playback speed, seeking, volume, and Silent mode.

What to expect
• Some music may remain, especially quiet background music behind speech, and voices can sound slightly duller. Musicless cannot guarantee completely music-free playback.
• Audio separation runs locally on your device’s processor and uses extra memory while active. Performance varies by device and video.
• If removal fails, the video pauses and stays silent so music does not play unexpectedly. Choose Try again or Reload page when offered, or Play original sound to continue with the original audio. Copy-protected videos (such as rentals) can only play with their original audio.
• On iPhone, toggle Musicless before entering native fullscreen.

Your audio stays on your device
Musicless processes only the audio already loaded by YouTube in Safari to separate voices during playback. It never uploads, shares, records, or saves audio, and provides no download or export features. No account is needed, and the developer receives no audio, browsing history, identifiers, or analytics.

Free and open source: https://github.com/ieasybooks/musicless
Privacy policy: https://musicless.ieasybooks.com/en/privacy

Musicless is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.
```

## App Privacy (Nutrition Label)

Select **"No, we do not collect data from this app"** (**Data Not Collected**):
- Audio is processed transiently in browser/device memory and never leaves the device.
- Settings (`autoStart`, `consented`) stay in Safari Web Extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`); an internal random handshake token stays in `chrome.storage.local`. None of these are accessible to the developer.
- No analytics, crash SDKs, advertising SDKs, accounts, or backend servers.

## App Review Notes (paste in App Store Connect)

```text
Musicless is a Safari Web Extension (with a minimal setup companion app) that removes background music from YouTube videos in Safari while preserving speech and singing. Everything runs locally on-device using a bundled ONNX Runtime Web WebAssembly runtime and Spleeter 2-stem model. No account, login, payment, or remote server is required.

How to test on macOS:
1. Launch the Musicless app and click "Open Safari Settings…".
2. Enable the "Musicless" extension and allow access to youtube.com, m.youtube.com, and youtube-nocookie.com.
3. A first-run welcome page opens in Safari. Click "Turn on Musicless" to opt in to local audio processing.
4. Open a public YouTube video with speech over background music, for example NASA Goddard's public-domain video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nr5Pj6GQL2o (no YouTube login required).
5. After 1–3 seconds ("Removing music"), playback begins with background music reduced and voices preserved. Click the crossed-out music note button in the player bar (left of CC) to toggle between "Restore original sound" and "Remove music".

How to test on iOS / iPadOS:
1. Open the Musicless app to view the setup instructions.
2. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Musicless, turn on "Allow Extension", and allow youtube.com, m.youtube.com, and youtube-nocookie.com.
3. Open https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=nr5Pj6GQL2o in Safari. Tap the Musicless button (note with red dot) in the top-right controls to open the welcome page, tap "Turn on Musicless", then tap "Back to the video".
4. Playback starts with background music reduced and voices kept. Tap the crossed-out note button to toggle original sound.

Technical & policy notes:
- No media downloading, saving, recording, or export: Musicless only processes audio buffers already streamed by YouTube's own web player in memory for real-time playback. It does not block ads and does not bypass DRM/copy protection (protected videos fail closed and offer "Play original sound").
- All code, ONNX Runtime Web WASM, and the Spleeter 2-stem ONNX model are bundled inside the app extension; no remote executable code or models are downloaded.
- The macOS companion app keeps com.apple.security.network.client solely because sandboxed WKWebView WebContent XPC requires it to render the local setup HTML cleanly; the app makes no outbound network requests. The extension target has no network entitlement.
- Open-source repository: https://github.com/ieasybooks/musicless
```
